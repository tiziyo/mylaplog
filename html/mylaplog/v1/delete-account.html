What happens when you delete your account:

Deleting your account is permanent and irreversible. The following records will be permanently purged from our database:

  • Your driver identity, login credentials, and linked Google / Kakao OAuth tokens.
  • All recorded trackday sessions, dates, and track weather conditions.
  • All official lap times, personal bests, and sector 1/2/3 split records.
  • All 4-wheel cold & hot tire pressure setups, damper click calibrations, and chassis balance notes.
  • All registered garage vehicles and custom machine profiles.
